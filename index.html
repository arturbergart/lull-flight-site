Lull Flight is a calm companion for anyone who feels uneasy on a plane. It explains every sound and moment of your flight, helps you breathe through bumpy air and keeps you company until landing.

When it’s bumpy

One tap to calm.

The Turbulence button is always in the same place, under your thumb. Inside:

  • Slow breathing with the globe
  • A short film about bumpy air
  • Flying facts, each with its source
  • Gentle tips for bumpy moments
Always free

Your flight, step by step

Every sound, explained.

Takeoff, cruise and landing in short picture cards, and ten flight sounds in the order you hear them. Nothing surprises you.

Breathing

Breathe with the globe.

In as the globe grows, out as it settles. A slow rhythm that calms the body, with or without sound.

Always free

Rest on board

Rest to the cabin hum.

Cabin hum, soft rain or slow waves, made on your phone. Put on headphones and lock the screen: the sound keeps going, with a timer that fades out.

Before you fly

Weather on your route.

Official turbulence and storm warnings along your route right now, from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center.

A gentle distraction

Fly your route, calmly.

A slow little flight along your own route, and a quiz on the globe. A calm pilot medal waits at 100 points, and every real landing earns a city medal.
