Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to hello@lullflight.app. We read every message.

Restore my subscription

Open Lull Flight, tap the LULL logo at the top, go to the last slide and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel my subscription

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Lull Plus and tap Cancel. Apple explains it here: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Does it work in Airplane Mode?

Yes. Everything works offline except two things that need internet: "Weather on your route" (check it before you board; the result stays in the app for a few hours) and the weather at your arrival city on the medal card.

Sound stops when I lock the screen

Check that your iPhone is not in silent mode and that Lull Flight is up to date. Sleep sounds are made to keep playing with the screen locked and fade out on the timer you choose.

What does "Weather on your route" show?

The official warnings for turbulence and storm areas (SIGMETs) that are in force right now along your route, from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center. They cover the next few hours, not your whole flight, and they change through the day. Bumps are normal, and the plane is built for far more. Pilots get fresh reports during the flight.

How do city medals work?

Tap the plane button when you board. After landing, press and hold the round button at the top for 2 seconds. If you land earlier than Lull expects, hold it once more. With "Confirm landing by location" switched on in the flight sheet, Lull checks that you are near the arrival airport and asks "Already landed?" by itself. Your location is never stored or sent.
