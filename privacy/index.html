Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Lull Flight is built to be calm and private. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics. This policy explains the little data that is involved and where it goes.

Who we are

Lull Flight is made by Artur Bergart, Ellhornstraße 24, 28195 Bremen, Germany. Contact: hello@lullflight.app. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Artur Bergart is the controller.

Data that stays on your device

Your chosen routes, medals, quiz and game progress, settings and the last weather check are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, your medals and progress are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they survive a reinstall and appear on your other devices. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Lull Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details or Apple Account. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives a random anonymous app ID, your purchase history for Lull Flight and basic technical information (such as app version and device type). This is used to provide the subscription and to show us anonymous purchase statistics (for example, how many people start a free trial). See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Weather warnings on your route

When you open your flight sheet before a flight (with Lull Plus), or tap the weather icon during a flight, the app downloads the current public aviation weather warnings (SIGMETs) from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov, a US government service) and compares them with your route on your iPhone. Your route is not sent anywhere, and neither is your location, name or any identifier. Like any web service, aviationweather.gov receives your device's IP address in order to answer; see the National Weather Service privacy policy. Nothing else in the app uses the internet, except opening source links you tap.

Weather at your arrival city

When you open your flight sheet before a flight, or land, and your iPhone is online, the app asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the current weather at the destination airport, and Apple’s map service for that airport’s time zone. Only the airport’s coordinates are sent, never your own location. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy; we receive nothing.

Confirming a landing (optional)

If you have already allowed Lull Flight to use your location, the app checks it once when you land, compares it with the arrival airport on your iPhone and keeps only the answer "confirmed" on the medal. If you haven't allowed it, the app does not ask at that moment. It asks only if you mark a landing much earlier than expected. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere. You can say no, and the medal is yours either way.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking: your location is used only for the optional landing check described above, on your iPhone, and is never stored or sent. No access to contacts, photos or microphone.

Legal basis and your rights

Where GDPR applies, processing for purchases is necessary to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b)). You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are answered by deleting the app; for RevenueCat data, write to us and we will ask them to delete it.

Children

Lull Flight is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
